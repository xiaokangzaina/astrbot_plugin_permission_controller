# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AstrBot administrators who configure QQ group and private chat permissions inside the plugin settings page. They are usually checking bot access rules quickly, adjusting group or friend policy, and expecting state to persist between page reloads.

## Product Purpose

Provide a visual permission control console for the AstrBot permission controller plugin, including group/private target selection, permission policy editing, runtime status, weather context, theme controls, button sound effects, background music, and custom background audio upload.

## Brand Personality

Vivid, technical, and direct. The interface should feel colorful and energetic without hiding operational clarity.

## Anti-references

Do not reduce the page to a plain legacy settings form. Do not ship a generic AI-looking card stack, unreadable light-mode contrast, or a page where a background is merely added behind an otherwise unchanged layout.

## Design Principles

- Cover the full settings surface as one coherent console.
- Keep permission configuration visible and fast to scan.
- Use mixed vivid color intentionally while preserving contrast.
- Keep background music and button sound controls independent.
- Persist user choices so reloads do not surprise the administrator.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast in light and dark modes, keep controls keyboard-accessible, and respect reduced-motion preferences for decorative rhythm animations.
